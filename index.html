Lipa na M-PESA

Lipa na M-PESA lets customers pay for goods and services straight from their phones, with no cash needed. Merchants can accept payments through a Buy Goods Till Number, and organisations can collect bills through a Paybill Number. Every payment is confirmed by SMS, which makes record keeping simple and cuts the risk of carrying cash.

Pochi La Biashara

Pochi La Biashara is made for small and growing businesses. It keeps business money separate from your personal M-PESA balance, so tracking sales and expenses is easier. Customers can send payments directly to your business, and you can move money to your M-PESA account or pay suppliers whenever you need to.

Fuliza

Fuliza is an overdraft service that lets you complete M-PESA transactions even when your balance is not enough. It is useful in emergencies or when you are in a rush to pay. The amount you use is repaid automatically the next time you receive money, and fees apply, so it is best to use it wisely.
